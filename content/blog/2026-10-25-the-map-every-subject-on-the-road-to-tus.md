## Three parts

The [roadmap](/roadmap) is split into three parts, each with its own trail of subjects:

| Part | Subjects |
| --- | --- |
| Basic medical sciences | Anatomy, physiology, histology & embryology, biochemistry, microbiology, pathology, pharmacology, biology & genetics |
| Clinical medical sciences | Internal medicine, pediatrics, general surgery, obstetrics & gynecology |
| Minor clerkships | Neurology, psychiatry, dermatology, ophthalmology, ENT, urology and many more |

That's 30 stops and over 200 topics. A lot of paw prints!

## How progress is tracked

Every subject has its own page with a checklist. A finished topic gets a paw stamp and the date it was done, and it shows up under *Recent paw-prints* on the home page. When every topic of a subject is done, the whole stop turns pastel and gets a big check mark.

> [!NOTE]
> The order on the roadmap can change along the way. Everyone studies differently, and that's fine.
