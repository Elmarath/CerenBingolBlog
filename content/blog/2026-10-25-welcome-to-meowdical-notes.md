Hi, and welcome! I'm Ceren, a medical doctor preparing for TUS. This blog is my study diary, kept in the open: what I study, how I study it, and how far along I am.

<!-- truncate -->

## What you'll find here

- **Study notes**: how I work through each subject, the resources I use and the tricks that make things stick.
- **Plans & routines**: how I organise my weeks, and how I keep going on the slow days.
- **The roadmap**: every TUS subject as a stop on a paw-print trail.

## How the roadmap works

TUS has two tests, **basic medical sciences** and **clinical medical sciences**, and the clinical one includes a long list of smaller branches too. On the [roadmap](/roadmap), each subject is a stop with its own checklist of topics. Every time a topic is done, it gets a paw stamp and a date:

- [x] Set up the blog (2026-10-25)
- [ ] Finish the first subject
- [ ] Finish every subject

:::tip Follow along
New posts show up on the home page, and the trail fills up with pink paw prints as the topics get ticked off.
:::
